## ABOUT

_Enchanting Table RNG too frustrating? Alternative enchanting mods either too complicated or too overpowered?_



### Behold the **Enchanting Inscriber**!

The inscriber allows you to take your existing enchanted books and apply them to items _**without consuming the book**_. Simply place chiseled bookshelves around your inscriber and fill them with enchantments!



### Too expensive?

When enchanting costs are too high, use _**certain magical items**_ found throughout your adventures to bring the cost down!



### _certain magical items_

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>
  The "certain magical items" you can use to lower costs are as follows:
  
  - Amethyst shard
  - Trial Key
  - Blaze Powder
  - Dragon's Breath
</details>



## Cost Calculation
<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

  Enchantment cost is calculated as:

  **x** = _Enchant level_ | **n** = _# of ingredient slots filled_

  f(_x,n_) = 7 + x^(3) * ln(2.7 - 0.4n)

  ---

  TL;DR enchanting is EXPENSIVE, but using more ingredients yields increasingly better discounts

## Compatibility

This mod should be compatible with mods that either add new enchantments or modify enchantment compatibility behavior. If you run into any bugs, [report them on github](https://github.com/Benjammin-n215/BN-Chiseled-Enchanting/issues).
